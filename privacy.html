Privacy & data

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Blend In uses a small amount of room and player data to run the game and help you reconnect. Here is what the app handles.

What the game stores

  • Your display name and chosen avatar.
  • Room state and scores, plus clues and text chat sent in that room.
  • A random player session ID in this browser's local storage so the same device can reconnect.

People in your room can see your name, avatar, clues, and chat. Your word and role are shown privately to you during a round and revealed to the room at the end.

How long room data stays

Room records, including their chat and clues, are removed from the game's active store after 48 hours without room activity. The browser session ID stays in local storage until you use a Leave or Back to home action where available, or clear this site's local storage.

Leaving clears the session ID from this browser; it does not immediately delete the room record from the server.

Microphone and voice

Your browser asks for microphone access when you turn your mic on. Voice is carried through WebRTC to other players in your room. Depending on network conditions, audio may travel directly between players or through the TURN relay configured by the game operator.

The Blend In game server does not record or save voice audio. It does handle the signaling used to establish voice connections. Turn your mic off in the game or revoke microphone permission in your browser settings to stop sharing audio.

Services involved

The game is hosted by Render. The main game page loads typefaces from Google Fonts. Voice setup uses Google's STUN service and may use the TURN service selected by the game operator. These services may receive technical connection information, such as your IP address, as part of serving the site or connecting players; their own privacy terms apply to that processing.

Your choices

  • Keep your mic off unless you want to speak.
  • Leave the room and clear this site's browser data if you no longer want this device to reconnect as your player.
  • Use a nickname and avoid putting private or sensitive information in clues or chat.

For questions about a room, contact the person who shared its code with you.
